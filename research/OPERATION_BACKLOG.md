# Operation Backlog

For the next phase, StayJustIn is not focused on producing more articles first.

The focus is building the evidence library.

## Purpose

Turn old and new source documents into reusable intelligence:

- Facts
- Money
- Decisions
- Timelines
- Promises
- Resident impacts
- Unanswered questions
- Story opportunities
- Issue hub updates

## Default Processing Order

1. Add the document to `DOCUMENT_INTAKE_QUEUE.md`.
2. Decide the main issue folder.
3. Copy `templates/document-analysis-template.md`.
4. Extract the executive summary.
5. Pull key facts with confidence labels.
6. Pull every dollar amount.
7. Record decisions and timeline events.
8. Write resident impact in plain English.
9. Add unanswered questions.
10. Add follow-up opportunities.
11. Add reusable content ideas.
12. Update `REPORTER_NOTEBOOK.md` if the document creates a loose thread.

## Do Not Publish Yet If

- The main claim is 🔴 Unverified.
- The dollar amount is unclear.
- The vote or decision is uncertain.
- The document is draft language and not final.
- The issue needs a staff response before public framing.

## Ready to Publish When

- The core facts are 🟢 Confirmed.
- The resident impact is clear.
- Open questions are labeled honestly.
- Related documents are connected.
- The story has a clear public purpose.

## Research Editor Rule

A document is not finished when it is summarized.

A document is finished when its facts, money, decisions, questions, and issue connections can be reused without rereading the whole source.
