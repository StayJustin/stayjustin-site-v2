# Animal Services Research

Use this folder for shelter services, animal control, spay/neuter rules, microchip requirements, contracts, fees, and regional shelter questions.

## What to Track

- Animal shelter contracts
- Animal control service levels
- Fees and penalties
- Spay/neuter requirements
- Microchip rules
- Regional shelter arrangements
- Resident complaints or confusion
- Council votes and staff reports

## Open Questions

- Which agency is responsible for which service?
- What services do residents actually receive?
- What fees or penalties apply to pet owners?
- Are contracts clear and publicly understandable?

Animal Services, Shelter, Pet Rules, Contracts, Fees, Public Services
