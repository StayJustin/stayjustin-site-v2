# Budget Research

Use this folder for budget documents, budget amendments, transfers, spending authority, grants, donations, ARPA funds, Measure A, contracts, capital projects, and public spending questions.

## Priority questions

- What money was approved?
- Who can move or spend it?
- What public controls exist?
- What changed from prior practice?
- How does this affect residents?
- What still needs follow-up?
