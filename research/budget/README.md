# Budget Research

Use this folder for budget PDFs, financial reports, transfers, amendments, staff reports, spending questions, and Follow the Money research.

## What to Track

- Budget transfers
- Contract spending
- Department budgets
- One-time vs recurring costs
- Grants and reimbursements
- Capital improvement projects
- Reserve impacts
- Positions, staffing, and overtime

## Standard Files to Create

- `YYYY-MM-DD-topic-document-analysis.md`
- `YYYY-MM-DD-topic-fact-ledger.json`
- `budget-questions.md`
- `budget-timeline.md`
- `follow-the-money-notes.md`
