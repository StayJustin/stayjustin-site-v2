# Source Log Template

Use this to track where every source came from and whether it is safe to cite publicly.

## Source Type Notes

### Official Documents

Official documents can usually be cited, but still check whether the language is final, draft, proposed, or approved.

### Emails

Emails may contain useful leads, but do not publish private contact information unless it is already public record and relevant.

### Photos

Photos should be labeled by date, location, who took them, and what they show. Avoid publishing photos of private individuals unless there is a clear public-interest reason and permission when appropriate.

### Meeting Videos

Use timestamps. A video note without a timestamp is weak evidence.

### Resident Claims

Resident claims start as 🔴 Unverified unless supported by documents, photos, video, multiple independent reports, or official confirmation.
