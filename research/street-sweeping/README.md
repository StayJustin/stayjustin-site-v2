# Street Sweeping Research

Use this folder for street sweeping enforcement, signage, routes, parking impacts, warnings, citations, resident complaints, and council follow-up.

## What to Track

- Enforcement start date
- Warning period
- Citation policy
- Signage status
- Alternating-side sweeping areas
- Route maps
- Resident complaints by zone
- Council promises and staff updates

## Open Questions

- Were all signs installed before enforcement began?
- Which streets are still confusing or unsigned?
- When did warnings become citations?
- How many citations were issued after rollout?
- Did the city update residents clearly before penalties started?
