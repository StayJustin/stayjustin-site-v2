# Street Sweeping Research

Use this folder for street sweeping schedules, enforcement timelines, zone maps, signs, citation policies, resident comments, staff reports, and accountability tracking.

## Priority questions

- When does enforcement begin?
- Will warnings be issued before citations?
- Which streets are affected?
- Which signs are missing, outdated, or being corrected?
- How is the City communicating changes to residents?
