# Del Norte Research

Use this folder for the Del Norte housing project, park land dedication, field questions, traffic, parking, CEQA documents, conditions of approval, and resident concerns.

## What to Track

- Unit counts and project revisions
- Park land dedication
- Field retention and Little League impacts
- Traffic and parking concerns
- CEQA / IS-MND documents
- Fees, DIF, park in-lieu payments
- Conditions of approval
- Construction milestones

## Open Questions

- What exactly did residents gain and lose after approval?
- What field access or park commitments remain unresolved?
- How will traffic and parking be monitored after approval?
- Which conditions of approval are enforceable and when?
