# Reporter Notebook

This is not for publishing.

This is the loose-thread file for ideas, questions, patterns, gut checks, future investigations, and things that should not be forgotten.

## Active Threads

### Street Sweeping

- Ask whether all required street sweeping signs were installed before enforcement begins.
- Track whether warnings turn into citations and when that actually starts.
- Compare resident complaints by zone once enough reports come in.

### Budget / Follow the Money

- Track special events spending and whether the public understands what is included.
- Follow up on any budget transfers that appear vague or unusually broad.
- Watch for city manager reallocation authority and how often it is used.

### Fire / Public Safety

- Track Fire Station 1 funding path.
- Follow up on Fire Station 3 repair timeline and whether conditions were fully corrected.
- Compare promised repair timelines against actual completion.

### City Yard / Brandywine

- Track Surplus Land Act questions.
- Track whether Fire Station 1 funding is tied to City Yard sale assumptions.
- Watch for changes in unit count, price, or negotiation terms.

### Del Norte

- Track park fee calculations and whether residents understand what the city receives.
- Track Little League field questions after project approval.
- Watch traffic and parking concerns if construction moves forward.

### Athens Services

- Build a clean timeline of contract amendments and extension points.
- Track trash rate impacts and franchise fee terms.
- Explain why the 2048 date matters in plain English.

## Questions to Keep Alive

- What did staff promise, and where is the follow-through documented?
- What changed between the first version of a proposal and the final version?
- What costs were described as temporary but became recurring?
- What decisions were made before residents had a clear explanation?
- What items keep appearing in different documents under different names?

## Story Seeds

- Five budget items residents should understand before the next meeting.
- What West Covina residents miss when they only watch the vote.
- How one staff report can hide three separate stories.
- What a contract extension means years later.
- The difference between a city promise, a city plan, and a city payment.
