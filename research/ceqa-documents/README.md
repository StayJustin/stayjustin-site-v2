# CEQA Documents Research

Use this folder for CEQA notices, initial studies, mitigated negative declarations, environmental impact reports, mitigation measures, public comments, and development-related environmental findings.

## What to Track

- Project name
- CEQA document type
- Public review period
- Mitigation measures
- Public comments
- Traffic, noise, air quality, biological, cultural, and hydrology issues
- Responsible agencies
- Approval dates

## Open Questions

- Which impacts were found significant before mitigation?
- Which mitigation measures are enforceable?
- Who monitors compliance after approval?
- What public comments were submitted and answered?
