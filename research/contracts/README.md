# Contracts Research

Use this folder for vendor contracts, franchise agreements, amendments, extensions, scopes of work, professional service agreements, and procurement questions.

## What to Track

- Vendor name
- Contract amount
- Contract term
- Extensions
- Scope of work
- Bidding or waiver details
- Termination clauses
- Recurring costs
- Performance requirements

## Open Questions

- Was the contract competitively bid?
- What did the city receive for the money?
- What future costs or extensions are built in?
- What happens if the vendor underperforms?
