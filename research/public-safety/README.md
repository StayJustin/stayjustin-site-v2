# Public Safety Research

Use this folder for police, fire, emergency repairs, lawsuits, staffing, equipment, camera systems, overtime, and public safety budget questions.

## What to Track

- Fire station conditions and repairs
- Police and fire staffing
- Overtime spending
- Public safety contracts
- Lawsuits and claims
- Equipment purchases
- Camera systems and surveillance tools
- Emergency preparedness

## Open Questions

- What public safety problems are temporary, and which are structural?
- What repairs were promised and when were they completed?
- How much is overtime costing by department?
- Which public safety purchases create future recurring costs?
