# Council Agendas Research

Use this folder for agenda packets, meeting minutes, staff reports, consent calendar items, public hearings, resolutions, ordinances, and council follow-up questions.

## What to Track

- Meeting date
- Agenda item number
- Staff recommendation
- Fiscal impact
- Attachments
- Vote result
- Public comments
- Questions asked by council
- Items continued or pulled

## Open Questions

- What did the staff report say plainly?
- What was buried in attachments?
- What passed on consent without discussion?
- What questions were asked, and what questions were not answered?
