# StayJustIn Research System

This folder is the working intelligence library for StayJustIn.

The goal is to turn official documents, meeting packets, staff reports, contracts, budgets, maps, emails, and public records into reusable research that can support articles, issue hubs, Substack posts, Facebook updates, videos, and future investigations.

## Research workflow

Every document should be processed into the same basic structure:

1. Executive Summary
2. Key Facts
3. Money
4. Decisions
5. Timeline
6. Resident Impact
7. Questions Still Open
8. Follow-Up Opportunities
9. Issue Tags
10. Confidence Notes

## Confidence labels

Use these labels when sorting claims or facts:

- 🟢 Confirmed: Directly supported by an official document, meeting recording, staff report, contract, agenda, or public record.
- 🟡 Needs Follow-Up: Mentioned in a source, but incomplete, unclear, or missing context.
- 🔴 Unverified: Claimed by a resident, official, commenter, or third party but not yet confirmed by supporting records.

## Folder logic

Research is organized mostly by issue, not just by meeting date.

Examples:

- `budget/`
- `street-sweeping/`
- `del-norte/`
- `city-yard/`
- `athens/`
- `parks/`
- `public-safety/`

A document can belong to more than one issue. When that happens, file the main summary under the strongest issue and cross-reference it in related notes.

## Publishing rule

The research comes first.

The article, Facebook post, Substack post, or video comes after the evidence is organized.
